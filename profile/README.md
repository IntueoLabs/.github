# AI products, model research and the engineering that connects them.

Intueo Labs Inc. is a Canadian AI technology company based in Vancouver. We build software for ongoing agent work and investigate model behavior in the context of practical business tasks.

## What we are building

### Intueo Platform

A customer workspace for configured agents, conversations, working memory and connected business activity. We build the interface and the engineering around it, with access, permissions and human review defined for the intended workflow.

[Explore Intueo Platform](https://www.intueo.ai/platform)

### IntueoLM

Our model-development programme studies grounded answers, clarification and read-only Platform workflows through task-specific adapter post-training and evaluation. Current experiments build on an existing open-weight base; they are not foundation-model pretraining from scratch.

IntueoLM is in development. There is no accepted public model release, downloadable weight package or IntueoLM inference endpoint. The research page documents the experimental approach, measured internal results, limitations and the decisions not to deploy the current candidates.

[Read about IntueoLM](https://www.intueo.ai/models/intueolm)

## Research and engineering interests

- Source-grounded responses and traceable evaluation.
- English/French task design, data review and clarification behavior.
- Stateful agent systems, persistent context and usable customer interfaces.
- Account-scoped reads, application-enforced permissions and human review.
- Reproducible experiments with explicit acceptance criteria and preserved failure analysis.

Model behavior, persistent memory, authorized tool execution and the customer-facing product are distinct layers. Platform availability does not mean its current agents run IntueoLM.
